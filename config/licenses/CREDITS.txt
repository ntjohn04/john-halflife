John Half-Life
==============

Made by John. Free, not for sale (Half-Life-SDK.txt). https://github.com/ntjohn04/john-halflife

John Half-Life's own sounds and art: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Use them freely, crediting John Half-Life.


Built on
--------

* Half-Life (Valve); Opposing Force and Blue Shift (Gearbox Software). Needs Half-Life on Steam. Files your own
  games have aren't shipped: the first start takes them from your install.
* Half-Life Unified SDK: Sam Vanheer (Solokiller) and contributors.
  https://github.com/twhl-community/halflife-unified-sdk
* Half-Life Updated, Opposing Force Updated, Blue Shift Updated: the TWHL community. https://github.com/twhl-community
* Half-Life SDK: Valve, under its licence (Half-Life-SDK.txt).
* Xash3D FWGS's engine source: reference only for the engine's sound effects. None of its code is used.

Libraries
---------

Each licence is in this folder.

* OpenAL Soft 1.22.2 (sound): Chris Robinson and contributors. GNU LGPL 2 (OpenAL-Soft.txt). A separate library,
  cl_dlls/openal-hlu.dll (libopenal-hlu.so.1 on Linux), which you may replace. Source:
  https://github.com/kcat/openal-soft (tag 1.22.2). Only its library name is changed
  (vcpkg_config/registry/ports/openal-soft in this game's source).
* fmt, spdlog, nlohmann/json, JSON schema validator: MIT (fmt.txt, spdlog.txt, nlohmann-json.txt,
  json-schema-validator.txt).
* EASTL, EABase: Electronic Arts. BSD 3-Clause (EASTL.txt, EABase.txt).
* AngelScript: Andreas Jonsson. zlib (AngelScript.txt).
* libnyquist: Dimitri Diakopoulos. BSD 2-Clause (libnyquist.txt). Its codecs: FLAC, Opus, Musepack, libogg,
  libvorbis, WavPack (BSD) and minimp3 (public domain) (libnyquist-codecs.txt).

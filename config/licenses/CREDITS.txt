John Half-Life
==============

Half-Life, Opposing Force and Blue Shift as they played in 1998-2001, on a modern base: the original look, sound
and feel, with long-standing bugs fixed and some optional effects. https://github.com/ntjohn04/john-halflife

Made by John. Free; not for sale (see Half-Life-SDK.txt).

John Half-Life's own sounds and art (its weapon sounds, the hornet splats and the other sprites and textures it makes
or ships) are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): use them as you like, crediting John Half-Life.


Built on
--------

* Half-Life (Valve), Half-Life: Opposing Force and Half-Life: Blue Shift (Gearbox Software). You need Half-Life on
  Steam to play; Opposing Force's and Blue Shift's campaigns need those games too. None of their files are shipped
  that your own games already have: the first start takes them from your install.
* The Half-Life Unified SDK, by Sam Vanheer (Solokiller) and its contributors: https://github.com/twhl-community/halflife-unified-sdk
* Half-Life Updated, Opposing Force Updated and Blue Shift Updated, by the TWHL community and their contributors:
  https://github.com/twhl-community
* The Half-Life SDK, by Valve, under its licence (Half-Life-SDK.txt).

Xash3D FWGS's engine source was a reference for how the engine's sound effects work; none of its code is used.

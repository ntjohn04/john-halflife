John Half-Life
==============

Half-Life, Opposing Force and Blue Shift with Valve's look and small, careful changes to how they feel.

Requires Half-Life, Opposing Force and Blue Shift on Steam.
Install: extract the zip into your Half-Life folder, then restart Steam.
The first start shows a black screen for a few seconds while it sets up.

Settings: Options > Multiplayer > John Settings. Every change can be turned off there.

Credits and licences: the licenses folder.
More: https://github.com/ntjohn04/john-halflife
